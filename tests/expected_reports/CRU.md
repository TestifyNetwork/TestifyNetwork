## Section 1 — Mission, Vision & Ministry Purpose

**Mission (verbatim):** "**Win, build and send Christ-centered multiplying disciples**" (Cru "About" page: https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html)

**Vision (verbatim):** "**Movements everywhere so that everyone knows someone who truly follows Jesus.**" (Cru "Vision" page: https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/vision.html; also repeated on https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html)

**Stated ministry purpose (summary, sourced):** Cru frames its calling as evangelism and discipleship (win/build/send), rooted in historic Christian doctrine and the Great Commission emphasis stated in its Statement of Faith (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/statement-of-faith.html) and organizational overview (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).

## Section 2 — Core Values

Cru publishes three "Guiding Principles" as organizational values:

1. **Faith** — "keeping our focus on God… we depend on His Spirit daily," citing **John 15:5** ("apart from Him we can do nothing") (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).  
2. **Growth** — commitment to maturity and "true discipleship… lasting character change… develop multiplying, caring leaders" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).  
3. **Fruitfulness** — measuring "impact—seeing people come to Christ, grow in Christ, and carry His message to the world… faithful stewards… greatest return" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).

## Section 3 — Scripture Anchors & Theological Framework

### Scripture anchors (explicitly cited in Cru materials)
- **John 15:5** is explicitly referenced in Cru's values section under "Faith" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).

*Limitation:* In the evidence retrieved for this run, additional specific Scripture "anchors" (lists of passages) were not found beyond John 15:5. Cru strongly emphasizes the Great Commission in concept, and its Statement of Faith ends with a Great Commission-style command ("proclaim the gospel worldwide and make disciples of all nations"), but does not cite a verse reference in the retrieved text (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/statement-of-faith.html).

### Theological framework (doctrinal commitments)
Cru's **Statement of Faith** presents an evangelical, historic-orthodox doctrinal framework, including (high-level summary, sourced from the Statement of Faith):
- **Scripture:** Bible as "inerrant… supreme and final authority" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/statement-of-faith.html)  
- **Trinity:** one God in three persons (ibid.)  
- **Christology:** Jesus fully God and fully man; virgin birth; sinless life; substitutionary atonement; bodily resurrection; ascension/intercession; return of Christ (ibid.)  
- **Anthropology / Sin:** historic fall; human corruption and inability to please God without regeneration (ibid.)  
- **Salvation:** "wholly a work of God's free grace… faith in Christ alone" (ibid.)  
- **Holy Spirit:** convicts/draws, indwells, empowers believers; received by faith (ibid.)  
- **Church & eternity:** believers called to assemble; conscious eternal destinies; bodily resurrection (ibid.)

## Section 4 — History & Founding

- **Founded:** **1951** (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html)  
- **Founders:** **Bill and Vonette Bright**; founded on the **UCLA** campus as "Campus Crusade for Christ" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).  
- **Organizational identity today:** Cru is described as the **U.S. arm** of Campus Crusade for Christ International (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html).  
- **Global footprint (reported):** Cru reports "ministry presence in **191 countries**" (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about.html). A Cru press release also describes "more than 16,000 staff serving in **190 countries**" (Cru press release page: https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/news/press-releases/cru/cru-names-david-robbins-new-president.html).  
  - *Note:* These country totals differ slightly (190 vs. 191) across sources/dates.

## Section 10 — Potential Red Flags

1. **Reduced public financial transparency due to no Form 990 filings (structural, not necessarily wrongdoing).**  
   - **What is verified:** ProPublica reports Cru is not required to file because it is designated a religious organization (https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/956006173). MinistryWatch reports Cru "has chosen not to file" and includes Cru's explanation about donor/missionary privacy concerns (https://db.ministrywatch.com/ministry.php?ein=956006173).  
   - **Why it matters:** The absence of Form 990s limits public visibility into executive compensation, related-party transactions, and consolidated functional expense breakdowns typically available for large nonprofits.  
   - **Mitigating evidence:** Cru points to audited financial statements available through annual reporting (https://www.cru.org/us/en/about/donor-relations/annual-report.html; https://www.jesusfilm.org/give/accountability/reports/).

*No other verified legal/regulatory/public-conduct red flags were identified within the retrieved evidence set for this run.* (Sources reviewed for this conclusion: Cru About/Vision/Statement of Faith pages; Cru leadership press release; Cru annual report landing page; ProPublica listing; MinistryWatch listing; Jesus Film Project accountability page.)
