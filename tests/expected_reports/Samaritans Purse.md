Samaritan’s Purse publicly affirms (summary of key points, quoted concepts drawn from the Statement of Faith page):

- **Scripture:** “the inspired, the only infallible, authoritative Word of God”  
- **Trinity:** one God in three persons  
- **Christology:** deity of Christ; virgin birth; sinless life; atoning death; bodily resurrection; ascension; personal return  
- **Salvation:** “Jesus Christ is the only way of salvation”; repentance and faith resulting in regeneration by the Holy Spirit  
- **Eternal judgment:** heaven for the righteous; “everlasting punishment in hell” for the unrighteous  
- **Holy Spirit:** indwells believers enabling godly living  
- **Church:** unity of believers; evangelism and discipleship responsibility of all believers  
- **Human life and sexuality:** human life sacred “from conception to its natural end”; marriage defined as between one male and one female; biological sex as God’s design
