## Section 1 — Mission, Vision & Ministry Purpose

**Mission (verbatim, as stated by the organization):**  
After sharing the story of the Good Samaritan, Jesus said **“Go and do likewise.”** “That is the mission of Samaritan’s Purse—to follow the example of Christ by helping those in need and proclaiming the hope of the Gospel.” (Samaritan’s Purse homepage)  
Source: https://www.samaritanspurse.org/ (mission line shown on homepage snippet in the research set)

**Stated ministry purpose (verbatim, organizational “About Us” framing):**  
Samaritan’s Purse describes itself as “a nondenominational evangelical Christian organization providing spiritual and physical aid to hurting people around the world…with the purpose of sharing God’s love through His Son, Jesus Christ…[and] serves the Church worldwide to promote the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ.”  
Source: https://www.samaritanspurse.org/our-ministry/about-us/  

**Vision:** A distinct “vision statement” is not clearly published in the sources captured for this report (limitation). The ministry repeatedly emphasizes offering **help and hope in Jesus’ Name** and advancing the Gospel (see “About Us” and “History”).  
Sources: https://www.samaritanspurse.org/our-ministry/about-us/ ; https://www.samaritanspurse.org/our-ministry/history/

## Section 2 — Core Values

Samaritan’s Purse does **not** appear (in the sources captured here) to publish a single, consolidated “Core Values” list (limitation). However, the following **stated** values and value-commitments are explicitly evidenced:

1. **Christ-centered Gospel advance** — “Our ministry is all about Jesus—first, last, and always.”  
   Source: https://www.samaritanspurse.org/our-ministry/about-us/

## Section 10 — Potential Red Flags

Verified, sourced items (proportional assessment; not rumor):
